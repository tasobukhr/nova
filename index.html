Our Mission & Story

Born from a Passion for Happier, Confident Dogs

Loose Leashes BarkAcademy was founded on a simple truth: dog training shouldn't feel like a battle of wills, expensive private bootcamps, or outdated dominance tactics.

We built this digital academy for everyday pet parents who want compassionate, science-backed guidance that fits naturally into their busy routines. Whether you have an eager rescue puppy learning leash manners or an anxious dog reactive to neighborhood triggers, our gentle, positive-reinforcement methodologies build deep mutual trust and lifelong manners right in your living room.
